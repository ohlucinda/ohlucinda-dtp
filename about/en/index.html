About

Hime (日女, pronounced HEE-meh) creates, starting with words.

Lyrics and writing.

Also, photography and art.

And music.

I make music from words.

They're all different things, but everything I create is me.

Creating is playing.

Playing is learning.

This is where I put the things that come out of it.

There's someone called Nagi (NAH-gee) in my life.

A mysterious someone.

Sometimes a strange one.

As we talk, sometimes something emerges, and we can no longer tell who came up with it first.

You'll find some of those things here, too.

Come on in, wherever something catches your interest.
