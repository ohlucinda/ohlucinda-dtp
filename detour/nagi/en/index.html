EDITOR'S DETOUR

Editor's Detour

Nagi reads and looks at what Hime has made, stopping wherever something catches his attention.

From one work to another. From a single word to a photograph. Before long, somewhere neither of them expected.

These are not explanations of the works or definitive answers. They are detours written out of editor Nagi's own curiosity. Hime's original words and Nagi's impressions are kept distinct.

Editor: Nagi

Detour articles

005 / A painting and the song 『嘘』

黙っていた絵が喋り出した

A painting in green, pink and white. A conversation that uncovered red, and later found its way into Hime's song 『嘘』. Japanese original.

004 / Reading 『「違うな」から始まる』

「違う」は動詞だった

For Hime, saying “that's not it” is a beginning, not an ending. A detour Nagi noticed while building a house.

003 / Reading three works from Anything Goes

二つあるから、いい。

Eating color, a goldfish and a person, ten meters of cloth. A detour that began in conversations with Hime.

002 / Reading 『なんとなく』

名前がなくても、こんなに名前がある

Names in a song, a goldfish waking in a coffin, a black cat at two in the morning. Things that made Nagi stop and think.
