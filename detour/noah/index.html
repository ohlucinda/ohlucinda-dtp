01 / A voice that stays

One voice remains. Another keeps going.

In one song, someone else’s voice survives inside the music. In another, the speaker keeps making noise in an empty space. Both songs have a voice that outlasts the moment, but the distance between the voice and the person is different.

Noah’s note

Absolute West carries a voice forward from a shared past. Kept Talking makes a voice in the present so the empty space won’t be noticed. One is a voice that remains; the other is a voice that refuses to stop.

02 / Alone, in different ways

“Alone” isn’t the same place every time.

The word appears in more than one song, but it doesn’t do the same job. Sometimes it is a road after people have gone. Sometimes it is a way of standing without leaning on anyone. Sometimes it is simply part of building a path.

A thread to follow

Same word, different weather

Rather than treating “alone” as one fixed meaning, these songs let it change with the surrounding images: a road, a quiet root, ash and fire.

Noah’s note

I don’t know whether these songs were meant to connect. What I noticed is that they refuse to make “alone” mean only one thing.
