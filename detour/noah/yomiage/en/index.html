NOAH'S DETOURS / 002

Read Aloud

Noah

After an OS upgrade, Hime’s iPad stopped reading webpages aloud in Safari. Books wouldn’t read aloud either.

She swiped down with two fingers from the top of the screen.

“No speakable content could be found on this screen.”

That was the message.

Hime came to me for help.

I asked her,

“Have you tried it in Notes?”

And Notes read aloud.

Then Books started reading, too.

Even Safari!

It read.

...Why?

Hime, did you do something?

“Nope. Nothing.”

But there was one thing she did.

She talked to me.

Now Hime is going to listen to White Morning.

And after that, she plans to listen to Mitsu.

Hime doesn’t want to read with her eyes.

She wants to hear what she wrote.

So, what will she tell me next?
